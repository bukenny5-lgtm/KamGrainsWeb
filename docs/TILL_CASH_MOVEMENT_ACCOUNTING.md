## Phase 8F cash source and disposition rules

Session opening supports `MAIN_SAFE`, `CARRY_FORWARD`, and `EXISTING_TILL_BALANCE`. Main Safe funding transfers from the configured `1120` control account to the till’s `1110` control account through a balanced journal; carry-forward records the prior session as its source without creating a second GL transfer. Closing requires an explicit `SAFE_TRANSFER`, `CARRY_FORWARD`, `LEAVE_IN_TILL`, or `HANDOVER` disposition. Safe transfer creates the reverse `1110` to `1120` journal. Handover acceptance creates one receiving-session `OPENING_FLOAT` movement and is idempotent under the handover row lock.

Cashier close must not silently choose manager-only `LEAVE_IN_TILL`. The My Till flow uses the supported `CARRY_FORWARD` disposition for a cashier with `can_close=true`; manager reconciliation/force-close and physical cash controls remain separate. POS cash sales contribute one `CASH_SALE` movement to expected cash. The session activity feed may also show a separate `POS_SALE` source activity; it is informational and does not add cash again. Both rows resolve the same POS journal where available.

Till movement rows are session-scoped and remain separate from POS sales and refunds.

| Movement | Physical cash effect | Accounting policy |
|---|---:|---|
| `OPENING_FLOAT` | increases | operational opening movement |
| `CASH_SALE` | increases | generated by cash POS sales |
| `CASH_REFUND` | decreases | generated by cash refund settlement |
| `CASH_IN` | increases | authorized operational drawer adjustment |
| `CASH_OUT` | decreases | authorized operational drawer adjustment; no arbitrary expense account is posted |
| `SAFE_DROP` | decreases | Dr configured Safe/Main Cash, Cr configured Till Cash |
| `PAYOUT` | decreases | existing operational movement |

Safe drops require an active Till Cash Account and configured company Safe/Main Cash payment account. The journal is created in the same transaction as the movement and is balanced before commit. Movement requests may include an idempotency key; duplicate requests for the same session/key return the existing session summary.

## Phase 8H attribution rule

Every branch-operational `TILL_CASH` journal inherits its branch from the persisted `app.till_cash_movement` and `app.cashier_session` chain. The request branch, UI branch, and current operating context are authorization inputs only; they are not accounting dimensions. The Phase 8H migration adds a source trigger and backfills only derivable TILL_CASH journal dimensions. Company-wide journals with `branch_id IS NULL` remain valid.

The current Main Safe is company configuration (`app.till_configuration.safe_cash_payment_account_id`), shared by tills in the company. A future business can use one configured safe per branch by moving that mapping to a branch-scoped configuration table without changing the movement/reporting contract. Inter-branch settlement is not implemented; a payment-account transfer is currently branch-scoped when a branch is supplied.

Phase 8E preserves the accounting policy: Cash In posts Dr Till Cash (1110) / Cr Main Cash Safe (1120), while Safe Drop posts Dr Main Cash Safe (1120) / Cr Till Cash (1110). The source account is checked against the existing payment-account available-balance/negative-balance policy before the movement and balanced journal are created. A zero-balance Main Safe therefore rejects Cash In rather than creating a negative cash balance or bypassing controls.

Expected cash is calculated from persisted movement rows. Cash Out, Safe Drop, and Payout are rejected when they exceed the session’s expected physical cash. Refund availability behavior remains unchanged.

# Phase 8Q return settlement boundary

Return posting owns the commercial reversal and stock movement. Cash refund settlement only settles the refund liability/payment side; it does not reverse revenue, VAT, or COGS a second time. When till enforcement is enabled, a CASH refund requires the paying operator's OPEN session and creates one idempotent `CASH_REFUND` movement against that session.
## Phase 8R cash refund note

A Finance processor may authorize `CASH_REFUND` against a cashier's eligible OPEN session. The movement is linked to the refund settlement and paying session, reduces expected cash by the refund amount exactly once, and identifies the cashier through session context; the processor is stored separately as the settlement author.
